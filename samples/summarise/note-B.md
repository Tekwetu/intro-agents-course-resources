**All-staff: changes to expense claims**
From: Finance · 2 September

From 1 October, expense claims must be submitted through the new portal rather than by email. Claims for September may be submitted either way. From November, email claims will not be processed at all.

Receipts are still required for all claims over £25. The £75 limit on client entertaining is unchanged.

Training sessions are on 15 and 22 September, both at 11:00. Attendance isn't compulsory but is recommended for anyone submitting more than two claims a month.

Questions to the finance inbox.
